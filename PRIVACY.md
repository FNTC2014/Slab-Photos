# Slab Plates privacy

Slab Plates is a personal Android app for photographing graded coins and listing them on eBay. It is shared privately and has no servers of its own.

**Photos and certificate details.** Photos are cropped, and certificate labels are read, on your phone. Finished plates and original photos are saved to your phone's Pictures folder. Nothing is uploaded unless you list a coin on eBay.

**eBay.** When you connect your eBay account, eBay gives the app a sign-in token, which is kept in the app's private storage on your phone. The app uses it only to do what you ask: read your shipping, payment and return policies, upload the photos for a listing, and create that listing. Listing photos and details go to eBay and nowhere else. The app does not store or share any other eBay data, and there are no analytics or tracking.

**Disconnecting.** Use Disconnect eBay in the app's eBay settings to delete the token from your phone. You can also remove the app's access from your eBay account settings at any time.
